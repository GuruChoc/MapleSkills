## Confidence policy

**COMMUNITY BASELINE**: current skill data plus reasonably strong community preset evidence.

**COMMUNITY DRAFT**: thinner, newer, incomplete or unsettled evidence.

Neither means official or guaranteed best.

## Feedback policy

Repeatable better-performing community results should update the relevant tree. MapleSkills should not preserve an older recommendation simply because it was there first.
